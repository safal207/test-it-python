# Career research course

`career` is the 23rd AI PATH programme. It adds career discovery to the existing 22 programmes without changing their IDs, scope or neural-course sequence. The four-week plan has four mentor meetings and a separately accessible syllabus and browser worksheet at `career-research.html`.

## Learning outcome

Compare three directions using 15–20 manually checked vacancies, record a short trial task in each direction, explain the choice and prepare a 30-day plan. This is a bounded sample for a personal decision, not a representative market survey, earnings estimate or employment guarantee.

## Evidence rules

- Keep the employer's wording, source URL, review date, publication date if known and the student's interpretation in separate fields.
- Count unique vacancies; tracking variants of one URL are duplicates.
- Compare similar geography, seniority and employment conditions. Do not pool salary amounts across currencies, periods or tax bases; an omitted salary is unknown.
- Check current availability by opening the vacancy. A cached search result or agreement between models is not confirmation.
- If the chosen assistant cannot search or read a page, supply the vacancy text, URL and date manually. Do not infer missing requirements.
- Codex, Claude, Grok, DeepSeek and Qwen are alternative tools for the research roles. Five paid accounts or five autonomous agents are not required. The website does not invoke them or ask for API keys.

## Browser worksheet

The worksheet starts empty, validates manually entered records and stores a snapshot on the device only after explicit saving. JSON and Markdown exports preserve evidence and unknowns. The page makes no research API calls and does not submit applications or contact employers.

The catalogue cover is conceptual artwork. `assets/program-covers/career.svg` is an authored educational diagram and must not be described as real vacancy data or a student result.
